Privacy and help

Keyboard shortcuts

Hold Right ⌥ to dictate anywhere. Inside Sloth, ⌘N starts a new note and ⌘J shows or hides the sidebar.

Global shortcuts

These work in any app. Change them in Settings › Shortcuts; Reset to Defaults puts them back.

ActionDefaultOn by default
Dictate (push to talk): hold, speak, releaseRight ⌥Yes
Dictate hands-free: double-tap to start, tap to stopRight ⌥ twiceYes
Cancel dictationEscYes
Start or stop meeting recording⌘⇧RNo. Turn on Meeting Recording.

Sloth listens for global shortcuts in every app, so pick keys other apps don't use. It warns you if the meeting shortcut is one many apps already use.
