Privacy and help

Privacy and your data

Sloth has no account, no telemetry, no update checks and no iCloud sync. Audio and transcripts stay on your Mac; meeting content leaves only when you pick a hosted summary provider, a hosted dictation provider, or turn on a feature that uses one.

What Sloth doesn't do

  • No account. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • No telemetry. Sloth is built without an analytics ID, and its analytics code does nothing.
  • No update checks. Sloth has no automatic or manual update check.
  • No iCloud or iPhone sync. Sync is switched off in Sloth, and its settings are hidden.
  • No meeting bot. Nothing joins your calls.

What connects to the internet

  • Model downloads. Speech models download from Hugging Face or Muesli's model mirror when you first use them.
  • Providers you choose. Summaries, and dictation if you choose a hosted provider, go to that provider. See the table below.
  • Calendar. Sloth reads calendars through macOS. Your calendar accounts sync the way they always do; Sloth doesn't connect to Google Calendar itself.

Where your data lives

WhatWhere
Meetings, transcripts, notes and dictation history~/Library/Application Support/Sloth/muesli.db
Settings, API keys and sign-insconfig.json and separate credential files in ~/Library/Application Support/Sloth, readable only by your user account
Saved meeting recordings (off by default)~/Library/Application Support/Sloth/meeting-recordings/
Notes and transcripts as MarkdownYour Sloth folder, ~/Documents/Sloth by default
Audio during a recordingTemporary files on your Mac, cleaned up if left behind
Speech models~/Library/Application Support/FluidAudio/Models, ~/Documents/huggingface/models (Whisper) and ~/.cache/muesli/models (some others)

Sloth never reads or writes Muesli's folders, so the two apps can sit side by side.

If your Sloth folder is inside a synced location such as iCloud Drive or Dropbox, that service syncs your notes. Sloth doesn't.

What leaves your Mac, per summary provider

Every hosted provider receives the same meeting material: the title, transcript, your jottings, the template, earlier notes in the thread, and on-screen context if you turned on Meeting context.

ProviderSent to
Anthropic (Claude API key)Anthropic, using your API key.
Claude Code (your subscription)Anthropic, through Claude Code, with whatever Claude Code loads from the run folder. With lookups on, the services behind Claude's connectors too. See How Sloth keeps Claude Code safe.
ChatGPTOpenAI, using your ChatGPT sign-in.
OpenAI, OpenRouter, Custom LLMThat service.
Ollama, LM StudioThe server at the address in Settings. By default that's on your Mac, so nothing leaves.
Apple Intelligence (on this Mac)Nothing leaves your Mac.

If a Claude Code run fails, the stand-in provider (Anthropic or Apple Intelligence) receives the same material.

Other features that can send text

  • Hosted dictation. Choosing OpenAI or OpenRouter in Settings › Dictation › Speech recognition sends your dictated audio there. The default, Local, doesn't.
  • Dictation cleanup. If you turn on AI transcript cleanup and pick a hosted Cleanup source, dictated text goes to that provider.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a meeting from its … menu. Its Markdown files move to .trash in your Sloth folder.
  • Settings › General › Clear meeting history removes all meetings, notes, transcripts and saved recordings from Sloth. Clear dictation history does the same for dictations.
  • To remove everything, quit Sloth and delete ~/Library/Application Support/Sloth and your Sloth folder.
