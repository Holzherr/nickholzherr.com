cooking idea

Make fresh pasta with the machine

4+ · 60-90 min

Tara does the flour well and cracks the eggs; kneading together. She turns the machine handle while you feed the sheet. Cut tagliatelle, hang on a chair back, eat for dinner.

Talk points: why the dough gets stretchy (kneading), counting turns of the handle, thinner setting = smaller number.

Needs: 00 flour, eggs, pasta machine, rolling pin, clean tea towel
