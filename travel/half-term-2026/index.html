End of October · three options

A week of sun after half term

Three quiet 5-star hotels by the sea for the week after half term. All have heated pools or 25°C days, and all are a short flight with no big time difference. The kids are 4 and 1.

Sat 31 Oct → Sat 7 Nov 2026 7 nights, dates can shift a day 2 adults + 2 kids Direct flights

This week is mostly term time: Tara would miss five school days (Mon 2 – Fri 6 Nov). Going in half-term week instead costs about £1,500–2,000 more, because flights to every one of these places are 4–9× the price that week.

Option 1 · Paphos, Cyprus · UK +2 hours

Cyprus

Almyra, Paphos

The best all-round fit this week. Almyra is a design-led hotel on the seafront in Paphos, walkable to the harbour, rather than a resort on its own. It has the warmest sea of the three (about 26°C) and heated pools with shallow play areas, and its kids' club takes children from four months, so both kids can go.

Option 2 · Canary Islands, Spain · same time as UK

Tenerife

Las Terrazas de Abama

Apartment-sized suites on the Abama estate on the quiet south-west coast, a long way from the Costa Adeje crowds we didn't like last time. It's the only hotel in the search that states its pools are heated all year (26–30°C), and it has the best flight times.

Option 3 · Canary Islands, Spain · same time as UK

Lanzarote

Princesa Yaiza · Royal Kids Suite

The closest to our ideal on paper: right on the beach at Playa Blanca, a suite with two real bedrooms and two bathrooms, and childcare for both kids. It costs the most of the three, and the flight home lands late.

Also looked at

Why not these?

Mauritius ~26°C, driest week

Back to LUX* Belle Mare would be lovely, and it's the driest tropical option that week. But the only direct flights are from Gatwick, which makes it about 29 hours door to door for a 7-night stay. Better for a longer trip.

Turkey ~£2,200

The cheapest by far as a Jet2 package, but the hotels are huge all-inclusive resorts. Not our kind of place.

Red Sea, Egypt ~£3,000

A bargain hotel with two bedrooms, but the worst flights in the search and a package-holiday setting.

Half-term week itself +£1,500–2,000

Every one of these routes costs 4–9× as much on 24 Oct, and some of the hotels had already sold out.
