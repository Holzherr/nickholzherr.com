Option 1 · Canary Islands, Spain · same time as UK

Tenerife

The Ritz-Carlton, Abama

The best all-round fit. The flights leave at lunchtime and bring us home by teatime, there's no jet lag, and the hotel sits on its own on the quieter south-west coast, well away from the Costa Adeje strip.

Why it's good

  • Best flight times of the three, both ways
  • Quiet, design-led 5-star with its own beach and pools
  • The Jet2 package is ~£900 cheaper than booking the hotel alone, and that's with flights included
  • No time change, so the kids' sleep stays the same

Trade-offs

  • Kids Camp is for ages 4–14, so the little one stays with us
  • About a 50-minute transfer from the airport
  • 22°C is warm, but not hot

Kids: Abama Kids Camp, 09:00–17:00, from €25 a half day, ages 4–14.

Option 2 · Red Sea, Egypt · UK +2 hours

Sharm el Sheikh

Four Seasons Resort

The warmest option by a few degrees and the driest: in ten years of mid-December weather records it never rained on these days. The Four Seasons is set apart from the busier package-holiday side of Sharm, and it's the snorkelling spot of the three.

Why it's good

  • Proper beach heat, with sea temperatures like Dubai's

  • No visa needed for Sharm stays up to 15 days

Trade-offs

  • We land home at 22:15 on the Thursday

  • Busier package-holiday feel outside the hotel
  • Kids club is 4–12, so the little one stays with us

Kids: Kids For All Seasons (ages 4–12), cots, and a shallow 60cm play pool that suits a toddler.

Option 3 · Canary Islands, Spain · same time as UK

Lanzarote

Princesa Yaiza Suite Hotel Resort

The cheapest and simplest. It's the only hotel we found with a baby club that takes the little one, so we could both get a proper break. The suites are on the beach at Playa Blanca, and the flights are early out and mid-afternoon home.

Also looked at

Why not these?

Dubai ~£4,700–5,300

Warmest direct flight option (26°C), but it costs nearly twice as much, the 5 nights shrink to about 4 days because of the flight times, and the Foreign Office has warned about Iranian strikes in the region since July.

Muscat, Oman ~£4,100–4,700

Beautiful hotels (The Chedi) and 27°C, but it's direct from Heathrow only and covered by the same regional warning.

Sanya, China ~£5,650

The only warm beach in China, but it's 16–18 hours with a change and 8 hours ahead. Better saved for a longer trip in February or at Easter.

Vienna ~£1,700–2,300

A different kind of trip: Christmas markets at about 5°C. The cheapest option, but no sun.

Prices were checked on 27 September 2026 on Jet2holidays, Google Flights, Google Hotels and Booking.com. Nothing is held or booked, so the prices will move.
