Priory Pass — Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Priory Pass shows your Edgbaston Priory Club membership entry QR code. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with the club.

What the app handles

  • Your club login (email and password) is stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device and is sent only to the club's member portal (myedgbastonpriory.com) to sign you in.
  • Your membership QR codes are fetched from the club portal and shown on screen. They are not stored or sent anywhere else.

What the app does not do

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts with us. We run no server and receive none of your data.

Deleting your data

Tap sign out in the app, or delete the app, to remove your saved login from the device.
