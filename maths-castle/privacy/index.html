Maths Castle — Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Maths Castle is a maths game for young children on iPad and iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: the game works fully without an account or a connection, there are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no in-app purchases, and nothing about your child is shared with anyone else.

Who we are

Maths Castle is made by Brambruesch Ltd, a company in the United Kingdom, which is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: brambruesch@nickholzherr.com.

Without an account

Out of the box, the app sends nothing to us. The castle's progress (the name typed on the first screen, coins, stars, levels, the treasures and friends bought, and recent rounds played) is saved only on the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

With a Maths Garden account (optional)

A grown-up can sign in, from the grown-ups area behind a parental gate, with a Maths Garden account (made by a parent on our website, not in the app). When signed in:

  • Sign-in: the account's email address and password are sent to our account service to sign in. The password is not stored by the app; the sign-in session is kept in the device's Keychain.
  • Levels: the app reads the children on the account and their Maths Garden levels, so the castle games start at the right level.
  • Progress sync: the castle save is copied to the account, one save per child, so it carries over between devices. It holds the castle's name (normally the child's first name), coins, stars, levels, what has been bought, recent rounds (which game, score, time), the sound setting, and a random identifier for the device. If no child on the account matches the castle's name, a grown-up can add the child's first name to the account.

We use this data only to sign in, show levels and sync progress (to provide the service you asked for). It is never used for advertising, profiling or selling.

Feedback (grown-ups only)

The app has a hidden feedback form for grown-ups. If you choose to send a note, we receive:

  • the text you type;
  • which screen the game was on and a summary of progress (coins, stars, levels, how many things were bought, the last few rounds);
  • recent error messages from the app;
  • app version, device model, iOS version, language and region setting, time zone and screen size;
  • if signed in, the account's user ID, the linked child's ID and the sync status (not the email address or names);
  • the country the request came from and a short one-way hash of the network address, used to stop abuse.

The form shows exactly what will be sent ("What gets sent") before you press Send. We use feedback only to fix problems and improve the game.

Where it is stored

DataWhere
Account, children, levels, castle savesSupabase (database and sign-in), hosted in the EU (AWS eu-west-1, Ireland).
Feedback notesReceived by a Cloudflare Worker, held there only until we collect them (usually within 10 minutes), then kept in our private development records.

Supabase and Cloudflare act as our processors and handle the data only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

  • Account data and synced progress: until the account is deleted.
  • Feedback: for as long as it is useful to fix the issue it reports; ask us and we will delete it.
  • Data on the device: until the app is deleted, or "Start again" is used in the grown-ups area.

Deleted data can remain in our providers' routine backups for a short time until those backups expire.

Children

Maths Castle is made for children aged about 3 to 5, playing with a grown-up nearby. Children are not asked for personal information: the only thing a child may type is the castle's name. Sign-in, account settings and links out of the app are in the grown-ups area, behind a parental gate; the feedback form is opened by a hidden gesture meant for grown-ups. The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking, and does not share children's data with anyone.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: grown-ups area → Account → Delete account. This deletes the account, its children and every castle save and level held for them.
  • By email: write to brambruesch@nickholzherr.com from the account's email address and we will delete it.
  • On the device only: grown-ups area → Start again, or delete the app.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within a month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
