Maths Castle

Counting games for little ones, on iPad and iPhone.

Eleven short maths games (counting, sums, number patterns, ten frames, dice and more) earn gold coins and stars. Spend them on treasures and friends for a watercolour castle, and open its rooms one by one. Princess Rosie shows how each game works, and the levels follow your child.

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking
  • Works offline, no account needed
  • Optional sign-in to sync progress between devices
